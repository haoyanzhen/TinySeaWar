# Aurora Trial Log

## Scope
- Character: Aurora
- Faction: Soviet
- MVP type: light cruiser
- Tier: 2
- Trial package: concept anchor, half illustration, skill cut-in, UI sheet, battle asset sheet, animation keyframes, VFX reference sheet

## Effective Direction
- Keep the silhouette calm and upright, with old cruiser funnels, mast lines, searchlight gear, and compact gun nodes.
- Use deep crimson, cold gray-black steel, and muted brass as the main palette.
- Treat support identity through searchlight beams, command glow, signal pulses, and circular support aura.

## Current Strengths
- Strong light cruiser readability through mast, funnel, small-medium guns, and searchlight motifs.
- The half illustration and cut-in preserve a steady veteran commander personality.
- Battle sheet separates searchlight, signal, gun, wake, and support-ring ideas clearly enough for later cleanup.

## Cleanup Notes
- Generated images are RGB reference sheets, not finished transparent sprites.
- Abstract any star-like ornaments into gear, crystal, or signal geometry before final import.
- Separate cut-in background light and searchlight effects from the character layer.
- Mark turret rotation centers, muzzle points, searchlight origin, and support aura center during manual postprocess.

## Retry Guidance
- If the character becomes too ceremonial, retry with more compact cruiser rig and clearer game-scale weapon nodes.
- If the faction motif becomes too literal, replace it with abstract ice, gear, and searchlight shapes.
- If animation frames drift, lock the same hair silhouette, coat hem, funnel position, and searchlight device across the frame sheet.
